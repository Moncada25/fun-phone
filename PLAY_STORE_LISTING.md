## English

Title: Fun Phone: Your private dialer

**Short description**

Private dialer, no ads: fast T9, contacts, widgets and optional tools

**Long description**

A fast, private phone app for Android: dialer and contacts in one place, with no ads, no analytics and no sign-up.

Calls
• T9 search by name or number
• Favorites, recents and missed calls at a glance
• Clear incoming and in-call screens; answer with a swipe or buttons
• Pick a default SIM or choose one for every call
• On-device spam filter: block hidden numbers or callers not in your contacts, and catch blocked numbers with or without the country code
• A custom call background for the people you choose

Contacts
• Quick actions to call, message, WhatsApp, email or favorite
• Tags, duplicate merging and a recycle bin to restore deleted contacts
• vCard import and export, plus an encrypted backup file

Optional tools: turn on only what you use
• Notes, checklists, reminders and voice notes, organized in folders
• Password vault with PIN or biometrics, two-step verification codes and autofill
• Expenses and budgets with a yearly summary
• QR scanner and generator, text to speech and speech to text
• Pomodoro, menstrual calendar, holidays calendar and paint
• A retro arcade with records and a scoreboard: Sudoku, Minesweeper, 2048, Snake, Breakout, Memory Cards, Hangman, Tic Tac Toe, Puzzle and Fun Bird

Make it yours
• Modern, Minimalist or Retro style
• Light or dark theme: AMOLED, dark gray or dim
• Accent colors, fonts and adjustable text size
• Home screen widgets for favorites, recent calls, notes and expenses
• Labels and navigation built for TalkBack and other accessibility services
• Lock the whole app with PIN or biometrics

Private by design: contacts, calls, notes and passwords are processed on your device. Accounts you connect, such as Google, may sync contacts under their own settings. Only two optional features go online: the holidays calendar asks Nager.Date for the country and year you choose, after your approval, and website or social previews load the site you open, which receives the request under its own policy. Speech tools use your Android speech provider, which may need a connection.

To get started, open Device Status after installing, grant the permissions and set Fun Phone as your default phone app. Some features depend on your device and Android version.

## Preview assets — campaign v4

- Canonical source: `screenshots/store/campaign.json` (stories, copy, per-capture style, theme
  and accent, composition theme). Real Compose UI captured on the Pixel 9 Pro emulator
  (1280×2856) with fictional names, notes, accounts and amounts, plus normalized fixture status
  chrome. Earlier campaigns were removed; git history keeps them.
- Contact photos are real portraits under the Unsplash License, bundled only in the benchmark
  capture host and paired with fictional names. Credits: `screenshots/store/CREDITS.md`.
- Phone masters: eight **1080×1920 RGB JPEGs** per locale in `screenshots/store/{es,en,de,fr,pt}/`.
- Feature graphics: **1024×500 RGB JPEGs**, `feature-graphic.jpg` in each locale folder.
- Raw native captures: `screenshots/store/raw/`. No real notifications, provider contacts or calls
  belong in these assets. The shell-only benchmark capture host uses its own fixture cache and
  refuses Contacts/Call Log permission; it must never preload or refresh the phone's provider.
  The incoming call is an in-memory fictional `UiCall`; no Telecom call is placed or received.
  If a home-screen widget of the benchmark package asks for Contacts during a capture, the script
  denies it.
- Review sheets: `screenshots/store/overview-{es,en,de,fr,pt}.jpg`.
- Editorial composition: one flat muted background and one accent per slide, serif headline with
  the second line in italics, no gradients, glows, badges or generated artwork. App UI and all
  typography are rendered deterministically.
- Identical compositions feed the website's ES/EN WebP set.
- All five languages have localized headlines, subtitles, native UI and fictional sample content.
  Play locale mapping: `en-US`, `es-419`, `de-DE`, `fr-FR` and `pt-BR`; Portuguese copy follows the
  Brazilian wording used by the app.
- No ranking, awards, testimonials, price badges, installation CTA, hands or branded device hardware.
  Headlines fit inside the top 20% of each image. Specs follow
  [Google Play preview-asset guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en).

Capture (isolated Community benchmark only; the script refuses non-emulator devices unless
`STORE_CAPTURE_ALLOW_PHYSICAL=1` is set):

```bash
./gradlew assembleCommunityBenchmark
adb -s <serial> install -r -t app/build/outputs/apk/community/benchmark/app-community-benchmark.apk
ANDROID_SERIAL=<serial> ./scripts/capture-store-previews.sh
# Or refresh only some languages:
ANDROID_SERIAL=<serial> ./scripts/capture-store-previews.sh de fr pt
```

The capture host accepts `scene`, `language`, `style` (`MODERN`, `MINIMAL`, `RETRO`), `dark`,
`darkStyle`, `accent` (a named app accent) and `number` extras; `campaign.json` lists every capture
and the taps needed to reach a state (two-step code, yearly summary).

Export from the sibling `fun-phone-web` checkout with `npm run render:store`. This reads the
canonical capture set and emits both Play masters and website assets; it does not publish.
From another checkout or worktree pass the source: `npm run render:store -- /path/to/screenshots/store`.
To export only some languages, add `--locales=de,fr,pt`.
The graphics are a review candidate. Release/Play predecessor gates still apply before uploading.

## Ready-to-paste listing text

`play-listing/` contains `title.txt`, `short-description.txt` and `full-description.txt` for all five
Play locales. The text is generated from the reviewed sections above; `play-listing/manifest.json`
maps each locale to its text directory, eight phone images and feature graphic.

```bash
python3 scripts/export-play-listing.py
./scripts/verify-play-listing.sh
```

The guard checks title/short/full limits, export freshness, native fixture coverage and the image
set for every language. Limits are 30, 80 and 4,000 characters respectively, per
[Google Play product-detail guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en).
The full descriptions consistently disclose optional social previews, holiday requests, Android
speech providers and account-managed contact synchronization. The 3.21.1 release notes above are ready for this local candidate; generating text does not upload it.
